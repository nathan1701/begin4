**What we found in Path B2:**
The energy system of Begin3.exe relies on a constant at virtual address 0x00464688
(in the .rdata section). This constant appears to be 4.0 and represents the 4:1
ratio between Weapon Energy Storage (WES) and Reactor Energy Storage (RES).

**Where it's used (3+ locations):**
1. 0x0040f871 - FMUL double ptr [0x00464688]  (weapon energy calc)
2. 0x0040f997 - FMUL double ptr [0x00464688]  (reactor energy calc)
3. 0x0040fc87 - FMUL double ptr [0x00464688]  (shield power calc)

All three are inside FUN_0040f4b0, the main ship status display function.

**Related game text:**
"Reinforced shields require 4x power" (0x004662f0) - confirms the 4:1 ratio is
intentional game balance design.
